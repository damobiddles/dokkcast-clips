Add an episode by choosing its zip from Ready to post. The clips, covers and captions are copied up, so whoever posts doesn't need the zip. Each episode gets its own private link: send it to whoever posts that episode, and they see only that episode. Adding the same episode again replaces it and keeps its ticks and link.

How to post
  1. Work through today's card, and any marked Overdue. Times are UK time unless the card says otherwise.
  2. For each platform, press Save video and, if the app lets you pick a cover, Save cover.
  3. Press Open to go to that platform's upload page. Upload the video, then press Copy caption and paste it in. YouTube also needs Copy title.
  4. Post it, or schedule it for the card's time.
  5. Tick Posted on that platform. Everyone sees the tick and your name.
  6. Don't change the captions. If something looks wrong, ask whoever sent you this link.
  7. Keep this link to yourself. Anyone who has it can see the clips and tick them.
