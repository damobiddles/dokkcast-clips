HOW TO POST THE CLIPS: {show}

Everything for this episode is in this folder. You don't need any other app.

BEFORE YOUR FIRST POST
- Ask whoever sent you this for access to the show's accounts: Instagram and Facebook (through Meta
  Business Suite), the LinkedIn page, the YouTube channel and TikTok.
- Keep this folder somewhere you'll find it again.

EACH POSTING DAY
1. Open kit.html in this folder (double-click it). It opens in your web browser.
2. Find today's post. There's one post per posting day ({days}), at {time} ({tz}).
3. For each platform shown:
   - Click Open (TikTok, Instagram, YouTube or LinkedIn) to go to its upload page.
   - Upload the video from that day's folder (named after the date and clip). The portrait video is
     for TikTok, Instagram and YouTube Shorts, and the square one is for LinkedIn.
   - Click Copy caption and paste it in. For YouTube, use Copy title for the title as well.
   - If the platform lets you pick a cover, use the "cover" image from the same folder.
   - Post it, or schedule it for {time}.
4. Tick Done next to that platform once it's posted or scheduled. The ticks are kept in this
   browser on this computer only.

GOOD TO KNOW
- Don't change the captions. If something looks wrong, ask before posting.
- No posts between 24 December and 1 January.
- If a video won't upload or a button doesn't work, each day's folder also has the captions as plain
  text files (captions.txt).
